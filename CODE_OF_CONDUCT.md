# Code of Conduct

OrbitPage should be a welcoming, professional place to collaborate on useful
self-hosted software.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while still addressing technical or interpersonal harm.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, private URLs, customer data, or
  unpatched vulnerability details.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Deliberate intimidation, disruption, or repeated bad-faith engagement.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This policy applies in OrbitPage repositories, issue and pull-request threads,
reviews, release discussions, and other project spaces. It also applies when a
person represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `contact@orbitpage.com`. Include links,
screenshots, dates, and relevant context when possible. Do not use a public issue
to report harassment, private information, or an unpatched security problem.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Reports will
be reviewed as privately and impartially as practical. Retaliation against a
person who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.

Security vulnerabilities follow the separate private process in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
