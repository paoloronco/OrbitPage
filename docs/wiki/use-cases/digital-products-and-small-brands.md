# Build a page for digital products and small brands

Build a small-brand page with product context, previews and a focused catalog. Check payment, protected delivery and support before sharing.

Part of the [documentation index](../README.md), under **Examples and use cases**.

## Build and publish

A small brand or digital seller often needs the context of a landing page and the transaction of a shop. OrbitPage keeps those jobs close without becoming a complex retail platform.

1. Introduce the brand and current offer in **Page** and supporting **Content**.
2. Add previews, formats, usage terms and support details before the purchase action.
3. Follow the **Shop** setup and catalog guides to configure payment, delivery files or service details and product availability.
4. Save and check the public page, then verify the purchase and delivery flow in the payment mode available for your edition before sharing a campaign.

<a id="problem"></a>

## A product grid rarely explains why the offer matters

Customers may arrive through a campaign, recommendation or social post with little context. A branded page can introduce the idea, show the useful details and place a concise catalog at the right point in the story.

<a id="structure"></a>

## Build the story first; let the catalog close it

Lead with the product promise and proof, then show formats, current offers, an email route and the Shop. Keep the number of visible choices aligned with what the team can support.

- Brand and current release
- Proof, previews or use examples
- Focused Shop catalog
- Newsletter, support and policies

<a id="ai"></a>

## Prepare campaign variants while keeping one visual system

AI can help revise launch copy, reorder supporting blocks, add a new call to action and propose page-theme changes. The owner reviews the planned edits and applies only the useful version.

Product specifications, prices, rights and policy language still require direct verification.

<a id="shop"></a>

## Move from secure checkout to delivery without a manual email

Create digital products with protected files or defined services with fulfillment and booking details. Stripe processes payment; OrbitPage records the order and provides the corresponding delivery route.

Use a specialist commerce platform when physical inventory, shipping, tax automation or a large multi-SKU catalog becomes central.

<a id="operate"></a>

## Keep offers, customers and page performance in view

Activate only current products, review orders and customer history, and use page analytics to understand how people reach the offer. Newsletter consent and support expectations should remain explicit.

## Check the result

- Follow the [Shop test checklist](../integrations/stripe.md#test-checklist), including the actual protected file or service instructions.
- Check formats, usage terms, price, currency and support details against the offer.
- Keep paid files in the private Shop file store; do not link their contents from public uploads.
