# Build a professional or studio page

Create a professional page with services, selected projects, proof and a clear enquiry route. Review booking and optional paid-service details.

Part of the [documentation index](../README.md), under **Examples and use cases**.

## Build and publish

Independent professionals and compact studios often need more context than a link list, less infrastructure than a full marketing site and a cleaner route from credibility to paid work.

1. Describe the practice and service area in **Page**.
2. Add services and selected projects in **Content**, explaining your role and using proof you may publish.
3. Add one enquiry or consultation action. Explain the scope before linking to a calendar, or complete Shop setup for a defined paid service.
4. Save, test every enquiry and booking destination, and review the public page before putting its address in proposals or profiles.

<a id="problem"></a>

## A profile should explain the work before asking for contact

A social biography or directory listing rarely provides enough room for focus, proof and a clear working relationship. OrbitPage combines those elements in a single, controlled reading path.

<a id="structure"></a>

## State the service, show selected proof, then offer the next step

Use the studio or professional profile preset, a concise service statement, selected work or information blocks, booking, location where relevant and contact.

- Role, focus and location
- Selected services or case work
- Paid consultation or enquiry action
- Professional profiles and contact

<a id="ai"></a>

## Shape a clearer service page from the expertise you already have

AI can prepare a shorter positioning statement, reorganize standard content blocks, add a focused call to action or propose a visual treatment that fits the practice. The professional reviews the page-level changes before applying them.

Proof, qualifications and outcomes should always remain grounded in real work.

<a id="shop"></a>

## Give a defined offer a direct payment and delivery path

Sell a paid consultation, audit, review or multi-session service with a booking URL and intake questions. Digital reports, templates or starter packs can use protected file delivery after Stripe confirms payment.

Keep custom proposals and complex scopes in the normal enquiry flow.

<a id="booking"></a>

## Embed the booking tool people already trust

Supported Calendar, Calendly and Typeform content can place availability or a structured enquiry next to the relevant explanation. Keep a normal destination link as a fallback when a provider blocks embedding.

<a id="domain"></a>

## A custom domain turns the page into a durable reference

For proposals, email signatures and printed materials, a domain controlled by the professional keeps the address stable. Search metadata, favicon and structured page content reinforce the same identity.

## Check the result

- Open the enquiry or booking destination without a dashboard session.
- Confirm that project examples, testimonials and client names are approved for publication.
- Check service scope, contact information and any paid offer against what you can deliver.
