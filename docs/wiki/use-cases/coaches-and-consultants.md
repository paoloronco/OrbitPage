# Create a coaching or consulting page

Create a coaching or consulting page that explains the offer, sets expectations and connects visitors to an enquiry, intake or booking route.

## Build and publish

Coaches and consultants need a page that explains who the work is for, what changes through the engagement and how a qualified visitor can begin.

1. Use **Page** and introductory **Content** to explain who the service is for and what it covers.
2. Describe the approach, session scope, duration and price when applicable, using verified proof and claims.
3. Connect an enquiry, intake or scheduling destination. Complete Shop setup before accepting payment for a defined service.
4. Save and review the public wording, form permissions and booking route before sharing the page.

<a id="problem"></a>

## A calendar link cannot explain the value of the call

Sending every visitor directly to availability creates weak context and poorly matched enquiries. A focused page can establish the audience, method, boundaries and expected next step before someone books.

<a id="structure"></a>

## Qualify with clarity before asking for commitment

Lead with the situation you help address, then show the approach, selected proof, offer options and a clear booking or enquiry route.

- Audience and problem
- Method and credible proof
- Single session or package
- Booking, intake and contact

<a id="ai"></a>

## Use conversation to turn notes into a tighter visitor path

Ask AI to condense an offer, reorganize the page, add a call to action or align the theme with an established practice. Review the proposal for tone, scope and factual accuracy before applying it.

AI edits the page; it does not coach clients, answer enquiries or make professional claims on your behalf.

<a id="shop"></a>

## Sell one session or a defined package with the right handoff

A service can include a price, booking destination, multiple sessions and intake questions. After payment, the buyer receives the fulfillment details and can continue into the scheduling flow.

Discovery calls, bespoke retainers and regulated services may still require a normal enquiry and suitability check.

<a id="measure"></a>

## Read interest by offer, source and next action

Use page and campaign signals to learn which positioning earns a qualified click. Order and customer views show purchases, while the booking system remains the source of truth for calendar availability.
