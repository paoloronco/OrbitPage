# Create a personal page

Create a personal OrbitPage with a clear introduction, selected links, photos and contact details, then check and share the public page.

Part of the [documentation index](../README.md), under **Examples and use cases**.

## Build and publish

You do not need a business to have a page. Use OrbitPage as a small home for the things you want to share, with one address you can put in a profile, message or email signature.

1. In **Page**, add your name, a short introduction and a profile image you may publish.
2. In **Content**, add your selected links, photos, interests and one preferred contact action.
3. Arrange the main action first, choose a readable **Theme** and save each edited section.
4. Open **Public page**, check every destination on a phone and share the same public address.

<a id="introduce"></a>

## A name, a photo and a few words are enough

Write a short introduction that sounds like you. Add a profile photo and the places where people can follow you or send a message. You can leave out a location or contact detail you prefer not to share.

<a id="share"></a>

## Share what you are interested in right now

Add weekend photos, a reading list, a playlist, a side project or your latest video. Put the things you want people to notice first at the top; move older content to another page when it starts to get in the way.

<a id="address"></a>

## Keep the link when your interests change

Share your OrbitPage address in a social bio, message or QR code. You can edit the page behind that address when you have something new to share, without sending a replacement link to everyone.

<a id="public"></a>

## Choose what belongs on a public page

Check your links and look at the phone preview before publishing. The page is public: use photos you have permission to share and keep private contact details, documents and information about other people off it.

## Check the result

- Open the shared address without signing in and confirm that the introduction and primary action are visible.
- Open each external destination and check that only information you intend to publish is exposed.
- Change a small detail, save and reload the same address to confirm that existing links remain useful.
