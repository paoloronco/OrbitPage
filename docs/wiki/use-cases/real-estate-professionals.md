# Create a property professional page

Build a property page with selected properties, local context and distinct viewing or valuation actions. Keep listings in their source system.

Part of the [documentation index](../README.md), under **Examples and use cases**.

## Build and publish

Independent property professionals need a credible public destination that can introduce the practice, surface selected listings and route each visitor toward the right conversation.

1. Set the professional identity and service area in **Page**.
2. Add selected properties in **Content**, with accurate facts, images you may publish and links to the maintained listing records.
3. Provide distinct actions for viewings, valuations and general enquiries, connected to the appropriate existing service.
4. Save, check the public destinations and review availability whenever listings change. Keep full inventory and client records in the specialist system.

<a id="problem"></a>

## Listings, valuation and contact often compete for attention

A large portal may hold inventory but says little about the local professional behind it. A social profile supplies personality but weak structure. OrbitPage can provide the editorial layer between discovery and the specialist property workflow.

<a id="structure"></a>

## Separate buyers, sellers and current opportunities early

Introduce the area and practice, show a small number of featured properties, then provide distinct routes for viewing requests, valuations and direct contact.

- Area, role and positioning
- Selected current properties
- Viewing and valuation actions
- Map, contact and professional profiles

<a id="ai"></a>

## Refresh the public story without automating the listing record

AI can prepare edits to page copy, standard image or link blocks, calls to action and the visual theme. Review addresses, availability, property facts and legal wording before applying any change.

A CRM or property portal remains the appropriate source of truth for full inventory and listing status.

<a id="shop"></a>

## Use Shop only for a genuinely paid, defined service

For most property pages, enquiry and viewing routes matter more than checkout. A paid relocation consultation, market review or digital guide may fit Shop, but listings and offers should not be forced into a product catalog.

<a id="measure"></a>

## Track the route into a qualified conversation

Compare viewing, valuation and contact actions by campaign source. Treat clicks as directional evidence, then evaluate lead quality in the system where the team already manages property relationships.

## Check the result

- Open each listing and confirm its availability, price and facts in the maintained source system.
- Test viewing, valuation and contact actions separately.
- Check permission to publish photographs and property details; keep client records in the specialist system.
