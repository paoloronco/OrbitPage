# Build a portfolio for projects and job applications

Build an OrbitPage portfolio with selected projects, your role, a public CV link and a contact route for applications or introductions.

## Build and publish

A job application, university project or freelance introduction often needs a little more room than a profile bio. Put a few examples of your work and a contact route at one address.

1. In **Page**, describe your focus and the role or project you want to pursue.
2. Add a small selection of projects in **Content**, with images, your contribution and links to the complete work.
3. Add a stable, public CV destination and a clear contact action. Use an additional page for an extensive project archive.
4. Save, check the public page and project permissions, then use its address in an application or introduction.

<a id="introduction"></a>

## Explain what you do and what you are looking for

Start with your name, your area of interest and a short introduction. A student can name their course and current projects; someone looking for work can describe the role or kind of project they want to take on.

<a id="projects"></a>

## Choose a few projects and explain your part

Use images, video and links to show the work. Add a sentence about what you made, your role and where the full project can be seen. A small selection with useful context is easier to read than an unfiltered archive.

<a id="contact"></a>

## Keep your CV and contact details easy to find

Link to a CV you are comfortable making public, a professional profile and your preferred contact route. Keep the file at a stable destination so the page does not send people to an outdated application document.

<a id="send"></a>

## Use the same address in your next introduction

Open the phone preview, check the project links and publish. Add the page to an application or email signature. Update it when a project finishes or your availability changes.
