## Dynamic QR codes keep your contact info current after printing

Choose dynamic QR codes instead of static versions to ensure your printed contact information remains functional. A static QR code embeds data directly into the pixelated pattern, which means any change to your phone number or email address makes the printed card or office sign obsolete. A dynamic QR code uses a URL redirect. The code contains a permanent link to a server that routes the scanner to your current digital profile. Because the pattern points to a database entry rather than fixed text, you can modify the destination at any time without changing the visual appearance of the code.

Use this redirection mechanism to maintain a sustainable digital networking strategy. It allows you to update your job title, social media handles, or branding assets instantly. If you transition to a new firm, you do not need to recycle physical assets or update the QR code displayed on your mobile lock screen. You simply update the profile associated with the redirect link. This ensures that every physical touchpoint, including printed cards, lanyards, or laptop stickers, remains a live portal to your most accurate professional data. This flexibility eliminates the recurring costs and waste associated with reprinting stationery every time a minor detail changes.

Verify that your digital business card provider offers dynamic functionality rather than simple vCard generation.
