# How to sell a digital download from a link-in-bio page

Sell a digital download from a focused public page by explaining the file, previewing its value and testing checkout and delivery.

OrbitPage · 2026-09-17 · English

[Read on OrbitPage](<https://orbitpage.com/en-US/blog/sell-digital-download-link-in-bio>) · [Browse the archive](<https://github.com/paoloronco/OrbitPage/tree/main/docs/blog>)

A digital file needs more than a purchase button. Buyers need to know what they are getting, whether it fits their task and what happens after payment. A link-in-bio page can provide that context before sending a visitor to a small, focused Shop.

<a id="offer"></a>

## Name the buyer's task and the file that helps complete it.

A useful product summary explains the format, what is included and who it is for. 'A budgeting spreadsheet with an example project and setup guide' is clearer than 'My ultimate template.' Show a preview that lets a buyer judge the structure without giving away private customer data.

Keep the page's surrounding copy consistent with the product description and current price. If the download requires particular software or skill, say so before checkout.

<a id="trust"></a>

## Put policies and support where a buyer can find them.

A small catalog still needs accurate seller details, product terms and a route for questions. Explain updates, file access and any limitations you can actually support. Do not promise lifetime access unless the delivery plan makes that credible.

OrbitPage Shop is designed for digital files and defined services on an eligible managed plan. The seller completes Stripe Connect onboarding and remains responsible for product information and buyer-facing policies.

<a id="delivery"></a>

## Follow the purchase path through to the file.

The public page should lead to the correct product, then to checkout and the post-payment delivery route. OrbitPage grants a protected download only after a verified payment; a browser return alone is not proof of purchase.

Before promoting the offer, use the documented test path to inspect product details, checkout state and delivery. Keep a support route visible for a buyer whose file or instructions do not work as expected.

<a id="fit"></a>

## Use a focused Shop for a focused catalog.

A few templates, guides or defined services can fit beside the page's story and proof. A large catalog, physical shipping or complex subscriptions need a more specialized commerce system.

Measure page clicks and completed orders separately. A product card may attract interest while the description, price or checkout step still needs work.
