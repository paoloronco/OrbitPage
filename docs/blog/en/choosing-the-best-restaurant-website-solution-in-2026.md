## Selecting Your Restaurant’s Digital Strategy

The restaurant website best suited for your business is the one that balances technical performance with the reality of your daily operations. To find the right fit, run through the following evaluation process to clarify your operational needs before committing to a platform.

5.  **Compare integration needs:** list the specific point-of-sale systems and third-party reservation widgets that must exchange data without errors within your new site’s architecture.
