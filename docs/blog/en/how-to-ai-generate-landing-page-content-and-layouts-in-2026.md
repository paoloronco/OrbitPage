Use prompt-to-component mapping to build landing page assets from text descriptions. When you enter product details, a Large Language Model (LLM) identifies necessary sections like hero headers, feature grids, or pricing tables. The system selects pre-coded modules from a curated library rather than drawing pixels from scratch. This process ensures the layout follows established design patterns and maintains responsive coding standards.

Adopt this methodology for rapid prototyping to prioritize speed and structural integrity. Since the AI utilizes a known set of building blocks, the output avoids the alignment errors and broken CSS common in earlier generative attempts. Cycle through iterations in minutes by swapping themes and copy styles to match your conversion goals. This workflow enables marketing teams to produce functional previews without a manual design phase.

Recognize the inherent ceilings of this approach for highly bespoke logic.
