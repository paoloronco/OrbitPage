### What is website management?

Website management is the ongoing process of maintaining a site’s performance, security, and content updates to ensure it remains functional and relevant. This includes overseeing the link infrastructure and hosting environments to prevent broken redirects and data silos. Professional management requires balancing technical uptime with strategic asset organization to support long-term business growth.

### How do I create a link for a website?

You generate a link by using a management tool to map a custom short URL or alias to a specific destination address. Once the redirect is configured in your dashboard, copy the generated link and paste it into your marketing materials or social profiles. Using a centralized tool ensures you can update the destination later without changing the published URL.
